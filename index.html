The problem it solves

Many small libraries still track books in registers. Finding a title, checking who has it, or working out a late fine takes time and leads to mistakes.

This system keeps one searchable record of books and members, and handles lending from start to finish.

Features

Six modules that cover everything a library needs.

📚

Book catalog

Add, edit and search books.

Find a book by title, author, ISBN or category. See copies available at a glance.

👤

Member management

Register and manage readers.

Each member gets an ID, borrowing limit and full history of past loans.

📅

Issue and return

Lend books in a few clicks.

Issuing sets the due date and lowers the available count. Returning restores it.

⏳

Fine calculation

Late fees without a calculator.

Fines are calculated per day late at the rate the admin sets.

📊

Reports

See what is popular and overdue.

View most borrowed titles, overdue lists and monthly activity.

🔒

Secure login

Separate admin and member access.

Only admins can change records. Members can search and view their own loans.

How a loan works

From search to return, in the order a librarian does it.

  1. 1

    Find the book

    Search the catalog and check that a copy is available.

  2. 2

    Pick the member

    Look up the member and check their borrowing limit.

  3. 3

    Issue the book

    The system records the date and sets the due date.

  4. 4

    Return the book

    The copy goes back to the shelf count.

  5. 5

    Collect any fine

    Late returns are charged by the day.
